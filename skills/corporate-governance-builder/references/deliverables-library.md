## 2. Document Request List

Request categories:
- Legal: commercial registration, bylaws, shareholder agreements, licenses.
- Strategy: strategic plan, business plan, OKRs, board presentations.
- Organization: org chart, headcount, reporting lines, job descriptions.
- Finance: budget, chart of accounts, approval process, payment process, audit reports.
- HR: handbook, salary structure, hiring process, performance process.
- Procurement: vendor list, procurement policy, contracts, purchase orders.
- Sales: pricing authority, discount rules, customer contracts.
- Operations: SOPs, service levels, quality reports.
- Risk/compliance: risk register, compliance obligations, incident logs.
- Technology: systems map, access controls, data policies.
- Projects: project portfolio, PMO reports, change requests.

## 3. Interview Guide Template

Fields:

- Interviewer

- Decisions owned
- Decisions requiring approval
- Main pain points
- Key reports used
- Current committees/forums attended
- Process/control gaps
- Suggested improvements
- Evidence/documents mentioned

Core questions:
- What decisions do you make independently?
- What decisions need approval?
- Where do approvals slow down?
- Which policies are unclear or missing?
- Where do responsibilities overlap?
- Which reports do you rely on?
- What issues repeat every month?
- What would you change first?

## 5. Governance Maturity Scorecard

Score 1 to 5:
- 1 = informal/ad hoc
- 2 = partially documented
- 3 = defined and approved
- 4 = implemented and measured
- 5 = optimized and continuously improved

Dimensions:
- Purpose and strategy alignment
- Board/ownership governance
- Executive decision-making
- Organization structure
- Delegation of authority
- Policies and procedures
- Risk management
- Internal controls
- Compliance and ethics
- Reporting and transparency
- Performance management
- Accountability culture

## 8. Delegation of Authority Matrix

Columns:
- Decision category
- Decision/activity
- Threshold
- Requester
- Reviewer
- Recommender
- Approver
- Final authority
- Consulted
- Informed
- Required documents
- System record
- Exception route

Decision categories:
- Strategy
- Annual budget
- Budget transfers
- Capex
- Opex
- Procurement
- Vendor onboarding
- Contract signing
- Hiring
- Promotions
- Salary changes
- Termination
- Discounts
- Credit limits
- Write-offs
- Bank payments
- Project initiation
- Change requests
- Policy approval
- Risk acceptance

Quality checks:
- No blank final authority.
- Thresholds are numeric where possible.
- Emergency exception route exists.
- Board-level approvals are not overloaded with routine matters.
- No person can request, approve, execute, and reconcile the same transaction.
