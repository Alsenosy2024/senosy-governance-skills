Use these as guidance, not as a rigid checklist.

- OECD/G20 Principles of Corporate Governance 2023: https://www.oecd.org/corporate/principles-corporate-governance/

## Quality Gates by Deliverable

### Current-State Report

Pass criteria:
- Uses evidence from documents and interviews.
- Separates findings from recommendations.
- Identifies root causes.
- Prioritizes gaps by risk and impact.
- Includes quick wins.

Fail signs:
- Reads like a generic essay.
- Only says documents are missing.
- Does not explain decision bottlenecks.

### Governance Model

Pass criteria:
- Shows clear governance layers.
- Defines decision rights and escalation.
- Distinguishes oversight from execution.
- Fits company size and maturity.

Fail signs:
- Copies listed-company board governance into a small founder-led company without adaptation.
- Adds committees with no authority.

### Organization Structure

Pass criteria:
- Supports strategy and operating model.
- Has clear reporting lines.
- Reduces overlap and bottlenecks.
- Supports segregation of duties.

Fail signs:
- Designed around people names only.
- Creates too many departments for company size.
- Leaves critical functions ownerless.

### Delegation of Authority

Pass criteria:
- Covers all material decision categories.
- Uses thresholds.
- Names final authority.
- Handles exceptions.
- Aligns with system permissions and procedures.

Fail signs:
- Vague terms like "management approval".
- No emergency route.
- Board approves routine low-value matters.

### Committee Charter

Pass criteria:
- Purpose is specific.
- Authority is clear.
- Inputs and outputs are defined.
- Cadence and quorum are practical.
- Minutes and action tracking are required.

Fail signs:
- Committee exists only to discuss.
- Same members sit on every committee with duplicated agendas.

### Policy

Pass criteria:
- Has owner, approver, effective date, review cycle.
- Defines clear rules.
- Links to procedures, controls, and consequences.
- Uses language employees can follow.

Fail signs:
- Legalistic text with no workflow.
- No evidence or record requirement.
- No exception process.

### SOP

Pass criteria:
- Starts from a real trigger.
- Lists roles, steps, approvals, controls, records, SLA.
- Matches the authority matrix.
- Can be followed by a new employee.

Fail signs:
- Pure narrative with no step sequence.
- Omits systems or forms.
- Contradicts authority matrix.

### KPI/KRI Framework

Pass criteria:
- Each metric has formula, owner, source, frequency, target, threshold.
- Includes performance, risk, control, and adoption metrics.
- Links to reporting cadence.

Fail signs:
- Too many metrics.
- No data source.
- No action if target is missed.

### Risk and Control Register

Pass criteria:
- Risks are written as cause-event-impact.
- Controls are linked to risks and processes.
- Control owner, frequency, evidence, and testing method are defined.

Fail signs:
- Generic risk list.
- Controls are slogans.
- No residual risk owner.

## Design Principles

- Fit-for-purpose over maximum complexity.
- Accountability before documentation.
- Decision clarity before process detail.
- Controls embedded in workflows.
- Committees only where collective judgment is needed.
- Policies must be enforceable.
- Reporting must drive decisions.
- Governance must survive people changes.

## Common Anti-Patterns

- Starting with job descriptions before structure and authority.
- Writing policies before understanding current processes.
- Confusing organization structure with governance.
- Creating a board pack without board decisions.
- Creating KPIs that measure effort, not outcomes.
- Treating risk register as compliance paperwork.
- Ignoring technology permissions.
- Designing governance that the company cannot operate.
