# Final study acceptance protocol

## Rule

Issue a final investment recommendation only when every applicable item is `Verified` or is immaterial to the verdict. An assumption may support a range or scenario; it cannot close a critical evidence gap.

## Required evidence by category

| Category | Minimum acceptance evidence | Blocker if absent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market and demand | Current primary/official data plus customer interviews, signed intent, pilot results, sales history, or equivalent direct demand proof | Yes |
| Customer and price | Primary customer evidence and current competitor price/offer benchmark | Yes |
| Site/channel | Signed/negotiable terms plus field visit, capacity/access/utility check, and traffic/catchment evidence where relevant | Yes if location-dependent |
| CAPEX | Scope/BOQ and at least three comparable vendor quotes for material packages | Yes |
| OPEX | Current lease, payroll, supplier, logistics, utility, and platform/service pricing evidence | Yes |
| Legal/tax | Relevant regulator requirements and local legal/tax review of material obligations | Yes |
| Operations | Capacity/process test, supplier feasibility, staffing plan, and critical technology/service confirmation | Yes |
| Funding | Funding source, debt terms if any, working-capital need, and downside liquidity headroom | Yes |
| Financial model | Formula audit, unit/date/tax checks, monthly ramp-up cash flow, and scenario reconciliation | Yes |
| Risks | 5×5 register, trigger thresholds, owners, mitigations, and stop/go gates | No, but mandatory content |

## Minimum scenario set

- Base case supported by verified drivers.
- Downside: lower volume/utilisation and adverse price or conversion outcome.
- Cost stress: higher CAPEX and variable/fixed cost shock.
- Delay stress: later launch/revenue ramp with associated cash burn.
- Funding stress: minimum liquidity and covenant/repayment pressure where debt exists.

## Recommendation language

- `GO`: all critical gates pass and the downside remains fundable within approved risk appetite.
- `CONDITIONAL GO`: economics work, but a named non-closed condition has an owner, evidence date, and stop rule.
- `NO-GO`: expected return, downside liquidity, legal feasibility, demand evidence, or execution risk does not meet the stated hurdle.
- `Finalisation Blocked`: an evidence gap prevents a defensible final decision. Name only the missing proof and how to obtain it.
