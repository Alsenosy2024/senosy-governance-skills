# Financial model integrity

## Required schedule order

1. Assumptions and revenue drivers
2. Revenue by channel, price, volume, and operating days
3. COGS and variable cost by driver
4. Payroll, rent, overhead, marketing, and other OPEX
5. CAPEX, pre-opening, deposits, contingency, and working capital
6. Five-year P&L
7. Cash flow and cumulative cash flow
8. Break-even, NPV, IRR, and scenarios

## Minimum reconciliation tests

- Channel revenue must equal total revenue.
- Revenue - COGS = gross profit.
- Gross profit - operating expenses = EBITDA.
- EBITDA - depreciation/amortisation = EBIT.
- EBIT - tax/zakat/finance costs = net profit where applicable.
- Opening cash + operating cash flow + financing - CAPEX - working-capital movement = closing cash.
- Payback must be calculated from cumulative cash flow; discounted payback from discounted cash flow.
- NPV must use the stated discount rate and matching period. IRR must use the same cash-flow series.

## Scenario discipline

Use conservative, base, and optimistic cases. Sensitise the drivers with the greatest elasticity, normally volume/utilisation, price/AOV, gross margin/COGS, payroll, rent, conversion, or capex. Avoid changing every driver in the same direction without explaining why.

## Discount rate and tax

State whether the model is project, equity, pre-tax, or post-tax. State the ownership and tax/zakat treatment. If neither a WACC nor funding terms are known, use a clearly labelled consultant discount-rate range and show its NPV sensitivity.
